# Extension UI decisions

The player remains the main surface. Matchup answers two questions: how the fantasy matchup stands, and which rostered players are in the selected game.

Use the app's dark green background and warm yellow accent. Yellow identifies the user's scoring and primary actions; opponent points remain neutral. Use tabular score digits, readable labels, and simple roster rows. Extra player detail opens only on request. Avoid placeholder avatar tiles, decorative icons, badges, gradients, glow, and nested cards. Keep meaningful borders and native form controls where they aid use.

Settings use aligned labels and controls with section dividers. The page has no hero, marketing copy, feature tiles or decoration. Compact controls still retain visible focus and narrow-screen layouts. The panel's scrollbar is hidden while scrolling remains available.

These are product decisions, not a blacklist of useful conventions. The supplied references criticize repetitive styling and weak hierarchy; standard accessibility and form practices remain appropriate.
